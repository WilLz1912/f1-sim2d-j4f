The '''Mercedes-AMG F1 W17 E Performance''', commonly known as the '''Mercedes W17''',<ref>{{Cite web |last=Mee |first=Lydia |date=5 January 2026 |title=Mercedes announces W17 livery reveal date and 2026 F1 season launch event |url=https://www.motorsport.com/f1/news/mercedes-announces-w17-livery-reveal-date-and-2026-f1-season-launch-event/10788127/ |access-date=24 January 2026 |website=www.motorsport.com |language=en}}</ref> is a [[Formula One racing car]] designed and built by the [[Mercedes-Benz in Formula One#Mercedes-AMG Petronas Formula One Team (2010–present)|Mercedes-AMG Petronas F1 Team]] to compete in the [[2026 Formula One World Championship]]. It is currently being driven by [[George Russell (racing driver)|George Russell]] and [[Kimi Antonelli]] in their fifth and second seasons with the team, respectively. Antonelli achieved his maiden win with the car at the [[2026 Chinese Grand Prix]].

As of the [[2026 Singapore Grand Prix]], the W17 has scored 11 wins (plus four sprint wins), 22 podiums (plus five sprint podiums), 11 pole positions (plus three sprint pole positions) and nine fastest laps (plus two sprint fastest laps).
