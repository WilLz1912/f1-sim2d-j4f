The '''Alpine A526''' is a [[Formula One car]] designed and built by the [[Alpine F1 Team]] to compete in the [[2026 Formula One World Championship]]. It is the sixth Formula One car entered by Alpine since rebranding from [[Renault in Formula One|Renault]], and the first car built by [[Team Enstone]] since the [[Lotus E23 Hybrid]] ({{f1|2015}}) to use a [[Mercedes AMG High Performance Powertrains|Mercedes power unit]]. It is currently being driven by [[Pierre Gasly]], in his fourth season with the team, and [[Franco Colapinto]], who begins his second season with the team after replacing [[Jack Doohan]] following the [[2025 Emilia Romagna Grand Prix|seventh round]] of the 2025 season.
