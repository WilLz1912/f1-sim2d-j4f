The '''Audi R26''' is a [[Formula One car]] designed and built by [[Audi in Formula One|Audi]] to compete in the [[2026 Formula One World Championship]]. It is currently being driven by [[Nico Hülkenberg]] and [[Gabriel Bortoleto]], both in their second season with the Hinwil-based team.

The R26 is the first Formula One car built by Audi after its takeover of [[Sauber Motorsport|Sauber]] and the first Hinwil-built car to utilize non-[[Scuderia Ferrari|Ferrari]] engines since the [[BMW F1|BMW]]-powered [[BMW Sauber F1.09]] in the {{F1|2009}} season. It is also the first Hinwil-built Formula One car to utilise [[Castrol]]-branded lubricant since the [[Cosworth|Ford Cosworth]]-powered [[Sauber C14]] in the {{F1|1995}} season and also the first Formula One car to carry the [[BP plc|BP]] and [[Castrol]] logos brands since the [[Renault F1|Renault]]-powered [[Alpine A524]] in {{F1|2024}}.
