The '''Racing Bulls VCARB 03''' is a [[Formula One car]] designed and built by [[Racing Bulls]] to compete in the [[2026 Formula One World Championship]]. It is being driven by [[Liam Lawson]] and [[Arvid Lindblad]], the latter in his first [[Formula One]] season.

The VCARB 03 is the first Faenza-built [[Formula One car]] not to use a [[Honda V6 hybrid Formula One power unit|Honda power unit]] since the [[Toro Rosso STR12]] in {{F1|2017}}, as well as the first car to use a Ford-branded engine since the [[Minardi PS04B]] in {{F1|2004}}.
